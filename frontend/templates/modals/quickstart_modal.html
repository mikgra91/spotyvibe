🚀 Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in a few simple steps — from setup to your first AI-powered playlist.

1

Setup

Enter your keys, connect Spotify, pick your theme.

2

Tell SpotyVibe what you love (and what to avoid).

3

Preview tracks, like the gems, dislike the misses.

4

Revisit any playlist — like, dislike, or dismiss.
