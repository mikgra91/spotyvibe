🚀 Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in 6 simple steps — from setup to your first AI-powered playlist.

→ → → → →
1

Setup

Enter your keys, connect Spotify, pick your theme.

Open the menu (☰) → enter your OpenAI API key, Spotify Client ID & Secret → Save. Click "Connect to Spotify" and complete the sign-in. Optionally adjust AI model, playlist size, and language in Settings. When the status pills turn green, you're ready.

Key Actions

  • ☰ Menu → 🔑 Credentials → enter keys → Save
  • 🔌 Connect to Spotify → complete OAuth sign-in
  • ⚙️ Settings → choose AI model, playlist size (optional)
  • 🌐 Language & 🎨 Theme — top bar (optional)

2

Build Your Profile

Tell SpotyVibe what you love (and what to avoid).

Expand Music Profile → create or select a profile. Fastest path: use "Describe Your Vibe" — write what you want in everyday language and the AI structures it for you. Or fill in Core Description, Must Have, Soft Preferences, and Avoid manually. Hit "AI Profile Update" to let GPT refine it, or "Save" to store it instantly.

Key Actions

  • Expand "Music Profile" → click "+ Create new Profile"
  • ✏️ Describe Your Vibe — free-text, AI auto-classifies
  • 📝 Or fill Core Description, Must Have, Soft Preferences, Avoid
  • 🤖 "AI Profile Update" = GPT refines · 💾 "Save" = instant, no cost

3

Generate a Playlist

Pick a mode, set optional filters, hit Generate.

Expand Discover Music → choose a playlist mode (Default / Create new / Append / Replace). Set optional audio filters for energy, tempo, valence and more — or use Band/Song Analysis to auto-fill them. Click "Generate & Create Playlist" — track cards appear as they're found. You can cancel early or use partial results at any time.

Key Actions

  • Expand "Discover Music" in the Spotify section
  • 🎵 Choose playlist mode — Default / New / Append / Replace
  • 🎛️ Set audio filters — energy, valence, tempo, … (optional)
  • ▶️ Click "Generate & Create Playlist" — watch tracks appear

4

Review & Feedback

Preview tracks, like the gems, dislike the misses.

Click any album art to open a ~30 s Spotify preview. Use ‹/› arrows to browse. Like (👍) with an optional reason to improve future picks. Dislike (👎) to teach SpotyVibe what to avoid. Remove (✕) for a neutral removal with no profile impact. Each card also has quick links to open the track, artist, or album in Spotify.

Key Actions

  • 🖼️ Click album art → ~30 s Spotify preview overlay
  • ◀ ▶ Navigate tracks with ‹ / › arrows
  • 👍 Like — improves future suggestions (optional reason)
  • 👎 Dislike — teaches what to avoid (optional reason)
  • ✕ Remove — neutral, no profile impact

5

Refine Existing Playlists

Revisit any playlist — like, dislike, or dismiss.

Expand Refine Playlist → select a playlist from the dropdown → Load Playlist. Like (👍) keeps the track and records positive feedback. Dislike (👎) removes it and records negative feedback. Dismiss (✕) removes without affecting your profile. The playlist is cleaned up and your taste profile is refined with every action.

Key Actions

  • Expand "Refine Playlist" in the Spotify section
  • 📋 Select playlist from dropdown → 🔄 Load Playlist
  • 👍 Like — keeps track, records positive feedback
  • 👎 Dislike — removes track, records negative feedback
  • ✕ Dismiss — removes track, no profile feedback

⟳

Repeat & Improve

Your profile learns. Next playlist is even better.

Every like and dislike sharpens your profile. Go back to step 3 and generate again — each playlist gets closer to your perfect mix. Use Band/Song Analysis to explore new artists, copy ready-made suggestions into your profile, and auto-fill audio filters from real data. Your run history keeps the last 5 generations for review at any time.

Key Actions

  • 🔁 Generate again — your profile makes each run better
  • 🔍 Band/Song Analysis — explore artists, auto-fill filters
  • 📋 Copy AI suggestions directly into your profile
  • 📜 Run History — review the last 5 generation runs
