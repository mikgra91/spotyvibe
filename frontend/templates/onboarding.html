Connect a Spotify app
SpotyVibe talks to Spotify through your own free developer app. It takes about 2 minutes.
✓ Client ID — OK
You'll paste this after creating your app on Spotify's dashboard.
✓ Client Secret — OK
The secret shown right next to your Client ID.
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard
  2. Click "Create app". Any name, any description.
  3. Set the Redirect URI to exactly:
    http://127.0.0.1:5000/callback
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret.
